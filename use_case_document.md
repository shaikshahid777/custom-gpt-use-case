# Custom GPT Use Case Document

## Project
**AI Job Application Assistant**

## Candidate Problems
1. **AI Job Application Assistant** — Helps students and entry-level job seekers analyze job descriptions, identify skill gaps, and tailor their application materials.
2. **Student Study & Revision Assistant** — Helps students organize study material, create revision plans, and practice questions based on their syllabus.
3. **Internship Task Assistant** — Helps interns understand internship instructions, break tasks into actionable steps, and track required deliverables.

## Selected Problem
**AI Job Application Assistant**

This problem was selected because job seekers repeatedly need to analyze different job descriptions, identify relevant skills, tailor their resumes, and prepare application responses. The problem is specific, recurring, and feasible to address using a Custom GPT.

## GPT Name
**AI Job Application Assistant**

## Target Users
### Primary Users
- Fresh graduates and college students applying for internships and entry-level jobs.
- Entry-level job seekers who need help tailoring applications to specific job descriptions.

### Secondary Users
- Career coaches and placement trainers.
- Internship mentors who support students during job preparation.

## Problem Statement
Entry-level job seekers frequently apply to multiple internships and jobs, but each application requires them to analyze the job description, identify relevant skills, tailor their resume content, and prepare application responses. Performing these repetitive tasks manually is time-consuming and can result in generic applications, missed requirements, and inconsistent application quality. A structured assistant is needed to help users prepare more relevant and consistent applications.

## Expected Outcomes
1. Reduce the time required to analyze a job description and identify relevant requirements.
2. Generate a structured skill-match and skill-gap analysis for each job opportunity.
3. Provide tailored resume bullet-point suggestions based on the job description and the user's existing experience.
4. Generate customized application responses or cover-letter drafts aligned with the target role.
5. Provide a pre-submission checklist covering job requirements, resume alignment, and application completeness.

## Use Case Validation
### Clarity
The use case focuses on the specific recurring problem of preparing and tailoring job applications for internships and entry-level positions. The target users and expected outputs are clearly defined.

### Relevance
Students and fresh graduates frequently apply to multiple opportunities and need to customize their applications. The GPT addresses repetitive application-preparation tasks.

### Feasibility
The use case can be implemented using standard Custom GPT capabilities such as instructions, uploaded reference documents, and conversational interaction. It can analyze user-provided job descriptions and resumes and generate structured recommendations and application drafts.
